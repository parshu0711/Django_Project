02Modules

Eight instruments, one console.

Each module replaces an email thread, a spreadsheet or a satellite phone call. Hover the drawings.

M-01Weather

Live telemetry

Temperature, wind, pressure, humidity and visibility from every automatic weather station, charted over 72 hours and seven days.

M-02Power

Power and renewables

Generator, solar and wind output against station load, with the renewable share for every reading.

M-03Fuel

Fuel and batteries

Tank level and battery charge, with alerts raised before the reserves run low.

M-04Crew

Crew and medical

Who is on station, in the field or in transit, and vitals and fitness for duty for each member.

M-05Stores

Stores

Fuel, food, medical supplies and spares with low-stock warnings and an audit trail that can't go negative.

M-06Resupply

Resupply

Ship, DROMLAN flight and helicopter shipments from Cape Town to the ice shelf, with ETAs on the dashboard.

M-07Maintenance

Equipment upkeep

Service schedules with overdue warnings, and work orders that update the equipment register when they're closed.

M-08Research

Research and field logs

Projects by discipline and daily field logs of activities, samples and the data collected.

03Signal path

From sensor to decision.

Readings leave the station gateway over the satellite link, pass five alert rules and reach HQ and the station team within a minute.

VSAT · bulk uploadRules · under 1 sEmail · HQ + leader
  1. S-01

    Station gateway

    A gateway PC at each station collects weather-station and power-plant readings, and buffers them while the link is down.

    Store and forward

  2. S-02

    VSAT uplink

    Readings go up in bulk with a station token. A batch that is resent after a dropped link is skipped, never duplicated.

    Token auth · idempotent

  3. S-03

    Alert rules

    Blizzard, extreme cold, whiteout, low fuel and low battery rules open an incident and email HQ and the station leader.

    5 rules · 6 h de-dupe

  4. S-04

    HQ and teams

    Everyone works from the same live deck: acknowledge incidents, plan resupply, schedule repairs and file SITREPs.

    60 s refresh

04Access

The right view for every role.

Sign-in is required everywhere. Every page and every API call is filtered by station, medical records stay with the people who need them, and a sensor token can only upload readings for its own station.

  • Light pages and one small refresh call a minute, for thin satellite links
  • The last known values stay on screen when the link drops
  • Every list exports to CSV for offline reports
