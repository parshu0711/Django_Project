Everything in one place

Built for life and work at minus forty

Each module replaces an email thread, a spreadsheet or a satellite phone call.

Live telemetry

Temperature, wind, pressure, visibility, generator load, solar, wind power, fuel and battery, charted over 72 hours.

Automatic alerts

Blizzard, extreme cold, whiteout, low fuel and low battery readings raise incidents and email HQ, with no duplicates.

Situation reports

Daily green, amber or red SITREPs from every station, with issues and requests to HQ.

Crew & medical

Expedition rosters, who is on station or in the field, and medical checks with fitness for duty.

Stores & resupply

Fuel, food, medical and spares with low-stock warnings, a full audit trail and ship or air shipments.

Equipment upkeep

Service schedules with overdue warnings and maintenance work orders that update the register.

Research tracking

Projects by discipline and daily field logs of activities, samples and data collected.

Open REST API

Station gateways push sensor data with a token and can upload in bulk after a link outage.

How it works

From sensor to decision in three steps

1

Stations send data

A gateway at each station collects automatic weather station and power-plant readings and sends them over VSAT. Readings are buffered while the link is down.

2

Rules check every reading

Alert rules flag blizzards, extreme cold, whiteouts, low fuel and low battery, open an incident and email HQ and the station leader.

3

HQ and teams act

Everyone works from the same live dashboard: acknowledge incidents, plan resupply, schedule maintenance and file SITREPs.

Secure by design

The right view for every role

Sign-in is required everywhere. Each page and API call is filtered by station, and a sensor token can only write data for its own station.

  • Works on slow satellite links: light pages, one small refresh call a minute
  • Keeps the last known values when the link drops
  • CSV export of every list for offline reports
